# Notes — One Star, Written in Sap

## Final entry (198 characters, 1 line)

> One star. I spent three years turning his sunlight into oxygen and he thanked me by watering me with leftover iced coffee and naming me Planty. Would not photosynthesize here again — signed, in sap.

The joke is built from the plant's own ledger: three years of oxygen (its labour) repaid with leftover iced coffee (bad water) and the name "Planty" (no respect). It opens like a real review ("One star."), keeps the grievance at the centre, and closes with a review-platform turn ("Would not photosynthesize here again") plus a signature "in sap" that nods at the bounty title and keeps the plant voice to the last word.
